About Windar

The Middle East subsidiary of MASTER Group Italy, supplying the Gulf since 2007.

WinDar Aluminium is a leading supplier of aluminium accessories for windows and doors in the Gulf since 2007.

As the Middle East subsidiary of MASTER Group Italy, we benefit from decades of expertise in engineering, material research and innovative product development since 1986. Our team is highly committed, competitive and service-oriented, ensuring maximum customer satisfaction and consistent quality delivery.
