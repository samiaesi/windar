Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This page explains which personal data Windar Aluminium receives through this website, why, and what you can ask us about it. We handle personal data in line with the applicable data protection laws, including the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

01 Who we are

This website is operated by Windar Aluminium, the Middle East subsidiary of MASTER Group Italy, which is responsible for the personal data described here.

02 What we collect

You can visit this website without telling us who you are: there is no account, no log-in and no payment. We only receive personal data that you choose to send us:

  • Contact form: type of request (business or technical), name, company, email, phone, country, project or product details and your message.
  • Email, phone or WhatsApp: the details you share when you contact us or one of our sales representatives directly.

Please do not send sensitive information (for example health or financial data) through the form.

03 Why we use it

  • To answer your request and send it to the right sales representative for your country or emirate.
  • To prepare quotations and technical answers, and to follow up on them.
  • To keep a record of our exchanges with customers and partners.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for marketing messages unless you have agreed to receive them.

04 Who can see it

  • The Windar team, and MASTER Group Italy when a request needs the manufacturer's technical support.
  • Service providers that help us run the website and receive messages: our hosting provider (Vercel) and Formspree, which delivers the contact form to our mailbox. They only process the data to provide their service.

Some of these providers may process data outside the United Arab Emirates. When this happens, we rely on the safeguards allowed by the applicable law.

05 What is stored in your browser

Windar does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website keeps a few settings in your own browser (local storage) so it remembers your choices; they are not sent to us:

  • windar-quote: the products you add to your quotation list.
  • windar-region: the country or emirate you chose in "Talk to sales".
  • windar-reader: the view you prefer in the catalogue reader (book or scroll).

You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

06 Content from other websites

To display the page, your browser also connects to other services, which receive technical data such as your IP address: Google Fonts (text fonts), cdnjs by Cloudflare (catalogue reader) and masteritaly.com (product pictures). The company video, when available, is played from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, only after you press play.

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, LinkedIn, MASTER Italy, Brital and the Big 5 exhibitions open their own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

07 How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only as long as needed to answer your request and follow up on it, and longer only when the law requires it (for example for accounting records related to an order).

08 Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to limit or stop its use, or to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to info@windar.ae with the subject "Personal data request". If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the competent data protection authority.

09 Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to the messages we receive is limited to the people who need them to answer you.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
